## The basics of Smasher 

Smasher is known as the most challenging antagonist or boss in Cyberpunk. He was employed by Hanako Arasaka's brother Yorinobu Arasaka, but previously had affairs with some criminal gangs in NC as we can learn from one gig associated with finding Johnny's porsche 911. It is presented in the original Cyberpunk 2077 game and doesn't have connections to Phantom Liberty. Smasher is the final boss in the game and should be defeated by V in order to get to Yorinobu Arasaka following V's agreement with Hanako Arasaka. The meeting with Hanako Arasaka marks the so called non return point, which implies that you won't get access to side jobs or gigs until you complete this one. Despite this name, after removing the chip by the Arasaka's surgeries, you will still have the opportunities to return to NC and continue from the point you met with Hanako Arasaka.

Smasher is known to have enormous physical power, strong armor, ultra-fast movements, fatal melee attacks, bursting cannon on his back, and powerful ICE (Intrusion Countermeasures Electronics) to inhibit his enemy's cyberdeck. The devastating gun on his back allows him to massively release deadly blowing projections leaving no chances to his enemies. Like his counterpart Sandayou Oda, he can move with incredible speed and strike deadly blows in the battle. But unlike Oda, Smasher is not able to restore his health during the fighting. Nevertheless, Smasher is not alone in the fighting. After suffering damage, Arasaka soldiers come to the battleground to protect their boss.

<img src="{{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/images/cyberpunk12.png" alt="">
Smasher's abilities: movement speed, deadly guns and ICE

To have enough chances to defeat Smasher when facing him, V should be skilled as much as possible, wear tier 5++ deadly guns, have high cyberware and armor points, etc. It is not possible to get this all completing only major missions without side jobs and gigs. Personally, before agreeing the deal with Hanako Arasaka and reaching Smasher, I completed all gigs and jobs in NC, including, the main storyline of Phantom Liberty. Thus, I was able to achieve maximum reputation level, street creds, 100% in all skills, more than 500 health points, tier 5++ components in cyberware, 40GB cyberdeck, etc.

## The Chrome

The chrome (cyberware) components define V's capabilities, including netrunning, caused damage and physical power. They depend on the character's skills and are developed simultaneously with them. Impressive netrunning skills aren't enough in case of Smasher, because the damage, which can be caused by quickhacks is low. The key components here are health points, armor ones and powerful guns. There are various chrome components, which can be installed achieving the necessary skills levels and each of them has its own characteristics. Ones significantly improve armor, other add health points and improve netrunning capabilities.

Since I've come a long way before got to Smasher, my cyberware had all tier 5++ components installed. They are listed below. One of the most useful is the second heart in the circulatory system. Because Smasher fires extremely dangerous projectiles, there is always a high risk of death even with the strongest armor. Second heart is something like insurance from an unexpected death. It doesn't guarante staying alive, but it helps to restore health in case if in one point something went wrong. As well as another component called blood pump that is a powerful health item. This implamnt instantly restores 45-110 health, after which it also regenerates 9-23 additional health per second for 6 seconds.

As well as other bosses, Smasher is vulnerable to quickhacks, which can be used against his chrome. But obtaining enough cyberdeck memory is a separate challenge of the whole game. The pic below shows the approximate memory size required for each quickhack to be applied on Smasher's chrome. Obviously, some of them can't be executed at all due to memory requirements, but the Overclock mode (E) might be an option there. Considering the Smasher's enormous power and the time required to restore cyberdeck memory, running even deadly quickhacks can't cause serious damage to him. In contrast, not deadly quickhacks such as cripple movement and weapon glitch can be very useful, because they can make him immobilize and open for straight shooting.

<img src="{{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/images/cyberpunk8.png" alt="">

Smasher posesses ICE (Intrusion Countermeasures Electronics), a device or technology, that allows him to identify enemy's location if it is not in his sights. It is also capable of blinding his enemy's cyberdeck, making it impossible to run scripts even with enough free memory slots. When applying counterhack, the enemy sees a bright flash, which also blinds them for seconds. In fact, memory size required to execute each script depends on the chrome configuration of each character.

## Guns

Personally, my fav guns are pistols, sniper rifles and shotguns. All of them are effective against Smasher considering the landscape of the battlefield. Shotguns have the most severe damage, but can be used only in conjunction with the cripple movement quickhack to immobilize the target before. It is useless to engage in close combat with Smasher having any kind of weapon, because his deadly melee strikes can't be stopped by any of them. Fast running, long or double jumps, the corresponding quickhacks help to keep distance with him.

Smasher also bears a deadly LMG (light machine gun), capable of ensuring continuous shooting for tens of seconds. The only way to protect from it is to find a natural obstacle and hide behind it.
